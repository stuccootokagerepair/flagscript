Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about flagscript and Make My Flag Script.

Is flagscript free to use?
Yes. flagscript is free and open source under the MIT license. You can download it, use it, and inspect the source on the GitHub repository.
Which Windows versions are supported?
The current build targets Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It ships as a self-contained application, so there is no separate runtime or framework to install.
What can I generate with Make My Flag Script?
You can build custom flag captures, spawn and teleport flag routines, and toggle hitbox markers. Everything is output as readable Lua that you can save, export, or reload from your script library.
Does it work offline, and where are my scripts stored?
flagscript runs entirely offline. Generated scripts and your saved library are stored locally on your machine, so you can reload them at any time without an internet connection.
